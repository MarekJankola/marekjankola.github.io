This thesis studies how to choose initial conditions that lead to a desired outcome in dynamical graphical models, with applications ranging from ferromagnetism to opinion consensus. It develops an efficient algorithm combining the backtracking dynamical cavity method with belief propagation reinforcement, at the interface of statistical physics and computation.
