This thesis investigates entropy production in periodically driven systems within stochastic thermodynamics. By mapping overdamped Brownian motion in a travelling-wave potential onto a time-homogeneous process, it derives an inverse thermodynamic uncertainty relation: an upper bound on entropy production expressed through the mean current and its fluctuations. The result also gives bounds on the efficiency of particle transport by a travelling potential, including optical conveyor belts.
