## Research

**Learning dynamics, identifiability and high-dimensional statistics.** During my first year at ISTA, I studied identifiability dynamics in shallow autoencoders, performative classification in high-dimensional Gaussian mixtures, and theoretically principled risk curves in statistical models for genetic data.

**Optimizing initialization in graph dynamics.** In my master's thesis at EPFL, I developed an efficient algorithm based on the backtracking dynamical cavity method and belief propagation reinforcement to find initial conditions leading to a desired outcome in dynamical graphical models.

**Entropy production in periodically driven systems.** In my bachelor's thesis, I derived and proved a new bound on entropy production in periodically driven systems within stochastic thermodynamics.
