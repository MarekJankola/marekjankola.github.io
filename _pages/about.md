<div class="biography" markdown="1">

I want to understand how neural networks extract causal structure from data and learn to solve tasks, and what their fundamental limits are.

During my studies in theoretical physics, I worked on non-equilibrium thermodynamics with [Artem Ryabov](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=bHT-1eUAAAAJ&hl=en) and explored connections between statistical physics and computation in my master's thesis with [Lenka Zdeborová](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/spoc/). From there, I drifted into modern machine learning and began my PhD at [ISTA](https://ista.ac.at/en/home/), where I am grateful to be advised by [Marco Mondelli](http://marcomondelli.com/) and [Francesco Locatello](https://www.francescolocatello.com/).

</div>
