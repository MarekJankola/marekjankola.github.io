I want to understand how neural networks extract structure from data and learn to solve tasks, and what their fundamental limits are.

During my studies in theoretical physics, I explored the connection between statistical physics and computation, with [Lenka Zdeborová](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/spoc/) as my master's thesis supervisor. I then turned my focus to modern machine learning and began my PhD at [ISTA](https://ista.ac.at/en/home/), where I am grateful to be advised by [Marco Mondelli](http://marcomondelli.com/) and [Francesco Locatello](https://www.francescolocatello.com/).
